iPhone Screen Repair Cost Guide (Strathmore & Calgary, 2026)

What actually drives the price — and the questions worth asking before you book anywhere.

Dr. Fixit Mobile · Strathmore & Calgary, AB · Updated September 2026

"How much to fix my screen?" is almost always the first question, and the honest answer is: it depends on more than most people expect. Here's what actually moves the price, so you know what you're paying for wherever you go.

1. Your iPhone model

Screen cost scales with the phone itself. A newer Pro or Pro Max model uses a more complex, more expensive display assembly than an older or entry-level model — this is by far the biggest factor in the final price, more than anything the repair shop does differently.

2. Part quality: OEM, premium aftermarket, or budget

Not all replacement screens are equal, even for the same iPhone model:

  • OEM / genuine-spec parts match the original display's color accuracy, brightness, and touch sensitivity most closely.
  • Premium aftermarket parts are a reasonable middle ground — noticeably better than budget parts, at a lower cost than OEM.
  • Budget parts are the cheapest up front but often show it in color accuracy, touch responsiveness, or True Tone behavior — and tend to fail sooner.

A quote that seems unusually low compared to others is worth asking about directly: which part tier is included?

3. What's actually damaged

A cracked glass layer with a fully working display underneath is a more straightforward repair than a screen with dead pixels, touch response issues, or display discoloration — those point to damage in the display module itself, not just the glass, which is a different repair.

4. Whether Face ID or other sensors are involved

On models with Face ID, the True Depth camera and sensor housing at the top of the screen have to be transferred carefully during a repair to keep Face ID working. A shop that isn't careful with this step can leave you with a new screen and broken Face ID — worth confirming upfront that this is handled properly.

Questions worth asking before you book, anywhere

  1. What part quality is included in this price — OEM, premium, or budget?
  2. Is there a warranty on the repair, and how long does it cover?
  3. Will Face ID and True Tone still work afterward?
  4. Is there a diagnostic fee if I decide not to go ahead?
  5. How long will the repair actually take?

At our Strathmore and Calgary locations, every screen repair comes with a 90-day warranty and our no-fix-no-fee guarantee — and diagnostics are always free, so getting an exact quote for your model costs nothing.

Because pricing depends so much on your specific model and the extent of the damage, we quote screen repairs after a quick look at the device rather than a flat number online — it's the only way to give you an accurate price instead of a guess.
